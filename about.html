OUR STORY

Modern ambition.
Proven experience.

An Iraqi business perspective shaped by practical experience and built for the future.

Kimora Iraq is an Iraqi company specializing in the supply of industrial chemicals and the delivery of integrated engineering and technical solutions for water treatment.

Although our commercial identity was officially launched in 2026, the people behind Kimora bring more than ten years of field, administrative, and supply-chain experience. We bring together the energy of a new beginning and the depth of established know-how to serve the evolving needs of Iraq's industrial and service sectors.

Working with local and international companies, we focus on reliable sourcing, responsive service, and a logistics and distribution network designed to reach projects and facilities across all Iraqi governorates.
